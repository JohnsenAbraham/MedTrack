About MedTrack

Bridging Patients and Accredited Physicians Seamlessly

MedTrack was created to bring institutional healthcare coordination into a modern, responsive environment. By integrating appointment scheduling with medication adherence tracking, we eliminate the administrative overhead common to modern medical offices.

Our platform enforces role-based access control (RBAC), cryptographic password hashing, and event-driven communication to ensure reliable, confidential healthcare management.
